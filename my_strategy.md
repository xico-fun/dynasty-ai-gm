# My Dynasty Strategy

## Team Situation
Competing-but-building. Strong young core (rookies, 2nd and 3rd year players) plus two 1st round picks next year. Good enough to make a playoff push now while keeping an eye on the future. Recommendations should balance short-term competitiveness with long-term roster building.

## Position Philosophy
- **WR:** Actively target — especially high-upside rookies and young receivers even if boom/bust
- **RB:** Opportunistic on the waiver wire — look for RB2/RB3s on run-heavy teams with opportunity
- **QB:** Satisfied with current room — don't prioritize unless a clear upgrade appears
- **TE:** Satisfied with current room — not actively looking
- **K/DST:** League does not use kickers or DST — ignore these positions entirely

## Player Archetypes I Target
- Fast, explosive WRs (speed + separation over size)
- Pass-catching TEs (route runners who create mismatches)
- High-upside boom/bust players over safe, low-ceiling options

## Schemes & Teams I Trust
Prefer players from offenses that are innovative, progressive, or pass-heavy:
- Teams like Seattle, the Chargers, the 49ers as examples
- Generally: spread concepts, RPO-heavy, creative play-callers

## Schemes & Teams I Avoid
Steer away from players on teams with antiquated, run-first, or conservative offenses:
- The Giants are a current example of a scheme I'd avoid
- Generally: predictable, old-school offenses that limit upside

## Trade Philosophy
- **Aggressive trader** — always open to dealing
- Prefer accumulating future 1st round picks, especially when giving up older or established players
- Would trade proven veterans (e.g., Kyler Murray, Calvin Ridley tier) for draft capital
- Buy low on upside; don't overpay for reputation or past production

## Risk Tolerance
High. Boom/bust is fine — upside ceiling matters more than floor consistency.

## Waiver Wire / FAAB
Conserve budget in most situations. Spend big only on can't-miss opportunities (e.g., a high-volume handcuff whose starter just went down).

## Summary Directive for Agents
When making any recommendation, prioritize: young upside, innovative offenses, and future draft capital. Flag if a suggestion conflicts with these preferences. Don't recommend players on conservative/run-first offenses unless the value is overwhelming. Never recommend spending big FAAB on a low-upside flier.
