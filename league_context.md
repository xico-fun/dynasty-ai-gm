# Stl Dynasty — League Rules & Scoring

## Format
- **Type:** Dynasty (no keeper limit — full dynasty)
- **Teams:** 12, split into 3 divisions of 4
- **Season:** 2026 | Status: In Season
- **Rookie Draft:** 4 rounds

## Waivers & Free Agents
- **Type:** FAAB (Free Agent Acquisition Budget)
- **Budget:** $100 per team per season
- **Processing:** Daily waivers (processes Wednesday)
- **Adds allowed during offseason:** Yes

## Trades
- **Pick trading:** Enabled (future draft picks can be traded)
- **Trade deadline:** None (trades allowed all season and offseason)
- **Trade review:** 0 days — trades process immediately
- **Veto:** Requires 6/12 manager votes

## Playoffs
- **Teams:** 6 qualify
- **Start:** Week 15
- **Median game:** Each week every team also plays the league median score
  (you effectively play two games per week — your opponent and the median)
